Privacy Policy

AnimeeDonghua Studio · last updated 25 October 2026

AnimeeDonghua Studio ("the app") is a desktop app for Windows. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @animeedonghua to manage that channel. This page explains what the app does with Google user data.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • YouTube (youtube): to upload the owner's videos, edit their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and delete videos that fail YouTube's checks;
  • YouTube Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read views, watch time and traffic sources of the owner's videos.

The app does not read the owner's email, contacts, or any other Google data.

Where the data is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). Video statistics are stored in local files used to plan future Shorts. The app has no server and does not upload this data anywhere.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. The only place it sends information is the owner's own Telegram bot, which sends the owner notifications such as a video's title, publish time and view count. No Google user data is used for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube in the app at any time, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
