AnimeeDonghua Studio ("the app") is a desktop tool for managing the YouTube channel @animeedonghua. It uses YouTube API Services.

YouTube Terms of Service

By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Videos uploaded with the app are subject to YouTube's rules, including its Community Guidelines and copyright policies.

Privacy

How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Use

  • The app acts only on the channel of the person who signs in, and only with the permissions they grant.
  • Every video is uploaded as private first. After YouTube's checks pass, it becomes public, unlisted or stays private, as the owner chose in the app. A video that fails the checks is deleted.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time (Connections (Ulanishlar) → YouTube → Disconnect (Uzish)) or remove access on the Google security settings page.
